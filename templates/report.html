2. Foundation Model Architecture & Physical Dataset Specifications

The Spectral Foundation Model uses an asymmetric 1D Vision-Transformer-style Masked Autoencoder (MAE) optimized for high-resolution 1D optical absorption profiles.

Transformer Encoder (Backbone Feature Extractor)

• Embedding Dimension: 256
• Transformer Blocks: 4 Layers
• Self-Attention Heads: 8 Heads (head dim = 32)
• MLP Expansion Ratio: 4.0× with GELU activations
• Patch Size: 10 channels (25 tokens across 257 channels)
• Total Parameters: ~3.6 Million

Lightweight Decoder & Downstream Head

• Decoder Dimension: 128 (2 Transformer Layers, 4 Heads)
• Mask Tokens: Learnable 128-dim vectors
• Downstream Head: Dual classification & regression heads with LayerNorm and GELU
• Masking Strategy: 25% patch masking (within the 15% to 30% guideline)
• Memory Efficiency: Zero-copy disk-backed memory-mapped streaming

Physical Dataset & HITRAN Optical Simulation Ingestion

• Total Ingested Dataset: 3,000,000 synthetic FTIR absorption spectra streamed across 100 HDF5 volumes (30,000 spectra per volume, 3.08 GB total memory-mapped storage).
• Wavenumber Grid: 257 discrete optical channels spanning 2083.33 to 7142.70 cm⁻¹.
• Molecular Simulation: Ro-vibrational transition lines from 5,336 HITRAN chemical components computed with pseudo-Voigt profiles and sinc Instrument Line Shape (ILS) convolution at 40 cm⁻¹ optical resolution.
• Target Monitoring Gases & Dynamic Ranges: CO₂ (0 to 1,996 ppm), CO (0 to 1,622 ppm), CH₄ (0 to 1,338 ppm), N₂O (0 to 766 ppm), and H₂O (0 to 24,865 ppm).

3. Self-Supervised Pre-Training Convergence

The model was pre-trained for 50 full epochs using AdamW with cosine learning rate scheduling on the official 3-million-spectra dataset with 25% patch masking. The composite loss combines Mean Squared Error with the Spectral Angle Mapper (L = L_MSE + 0.5 · L_SAM) computed exclusively on masked tokens to capture molecular ro-vibrational peak curvatures.

6. Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Robustness Analysis

Both models were evaluated under simulated sensor degradation conditions featuring 2× amplified Gaussian noise and random sinusoidal optical baseline drift (mimicking source temperature fluctuations and etalon fringes).

{{ robustness_chart_sfm | safe }}
{{ robustness_chart_baseline | safe }}
Physical Interpretation of Robustness Divergence:
• Classical PLSR Catastrophic Collapse (R²: +0.55 → -5.34): Classical linear chemometrics models such as PLSR compute static linear regression vectors directly across discrete wavenumber channels. When broad optical baseline drift or etalon fringes occur, the low-frequency polynomial slope is aliased directly into spurious gas concentration readings, resulting in catastrophic error amplification (MAE increases by more than 400%).
• Foundation Model Invariant Representation (Delta R²: -0.0028, Delta F1: 0.0000): The Spectral Foundation Model Transformer encoder relies on self-attention across localized 10-channel spectral patches. Combined with the scale-invariant Spectral Angle Mapper pre-training objective, the model learns relative multi-channel ro-vibrational band topologies rather than absolute photometric baselines, providing exceptional resilience against harsh real-world sensor drift.
